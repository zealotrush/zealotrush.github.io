So am I!

I don't think of myself as a complete novice.  Here are my _major_ strengths.

-   I can sight-sing using numbered notation (jianpu) -- but I don't know the staff notation.
-   I can play the piano one-handed, but just the melody -- I can't do the chords.
-   I have relative pitch, but no absolute pitch, I guess.
-   I am a professional audio algorithm engineer, and know frequency and pitch well.

And some _minor_ strengths.

-   I am good at fighting games, e.g., Street Fighter 2, and hardcore RTS games, e.g., StarCraft 1.  My APM (actions per minute) is 180+.  I mean, my fingers and the minds that control them are both strong.
-   My typing speed is fast.  ~180 characters per minute in Chinese with Wubi86.  I mean, I play _this_ keyboard very well.  I probably can play _that_ keyboard equally well.
-   I am a good learner.  I know what it takes, and how it feels, to learn.

However, I do have my weaknesses.

-   I am not a diligent person.  I _can_ learn well, but more often than not, I don't _want_ to.
-   I have too little time, and too many interests.  I play video games.  I play Emacs -- that's right, play.  I play AI.

With these in mind, here are my goals for learning piano.

-   Play it for fun.
-   Take it seriously.
